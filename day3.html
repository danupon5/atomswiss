Day 3 · Fri, 31 July 2026

Gornergrat → Riffelsee → Riffelberg → Brig

Gornergrat cogwheel train, Matterhorn views, Riffelsee hike, Brig old town

Hour-by-Hour Plan

  • 05:45–06:00

    🍽️ Wake and weather check

    Check Gornergrat webcam, Matterhorn visibility, wind, rain radar and trail conditions.

    Maintain the early train unless conditions are expected to improve shortly.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 06:00–06:30

    🍽️ Breakfast

    Eat the hotel's early breakfast or breakfast box.

    Drink water but avoid a very heavy meal.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 06:30–06:40

    🏨 Leave luggage

    Check out when possible and leave the suitcase with reception.

    Carry only a daypack.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 06:40–06:50

    🥾 Walk to Gornergrat station

    Walk to the Gornergrat Railway station opposite Zermatt station.

    Arrive early for a window seat.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 07:00–07:33

    🚂 Zermatt → Gornergrat

    Take the first summer cogwheel train to approximately 3,100 metres.

    Try the right-hand side for many of the Matterhorn views.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 07:35–08:25

    📸 Gornergrat summit

    Visit the viewing platform, Matterhorn viewpoint, Gorner Glacier viewpoint and summit area. Walk slowly because of the altitude.

    Have coffee or a light breakfast at the summit restaurant or self-service café.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • Approx. 08:25–08:40

    🚂 Gornergrat → Rotenboden

    Take the next downhill train one or two stops to Rotenboden.

    Check the electronic departure screen.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 08:40–09:25

    🥾 Walk to Riffelsee

    Walk approximately 10 minutes to the lake and allow photography time.

    Walk past the first viewpoint to find a quieter reflection spot.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 09:25–10:30

    🥾 Hike to Riffelberg

    Follow the marked trail Riffelsee → Riffelberg, approximately 3.1 km and one hour, mostly downhill.

    Use proper walking shoes. Do not hike in heavy rain, poor visibility or when feeling unwell.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 10:30–11:30

    🍽️ Brunch at Riffelberg

    Rest and have an early lunch or substantial snack.

    Choose a Riffelberg mountain restaurant for rösti, soup, pasta, sandwich, coffee or cake.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • Approx. 11:30–12:15

    🚂 Riffelberg → Zermatt

    Take the Gornergrat train back to Zermatt.

    Aim to reach Zermatt no later than 12:30.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 12:15–13:30

    ⏱️ Final Zermatt time

    Collect luggage, use the restroom, buy water and take final Matterhorn photographs.

    Have lunch only if you did not eat at Riffelberg.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • Approx. 14:00–15:10

    🚂 Zermatt → Visp

    Take a Matterhorn Gotthard Railway service down the valley.

    Leave Zermatt by approximately 14:30 at the latest.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • Approx. 15:15–15:45

    🚂 Visp → Brig

    Change to a short train to Brig.

    Keep an eye on the platform and connection time.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • Approx. 16:00–16:30

    🏨 Check in at Hotel de Londres

    Walk from Brig station to the hotel and check in. Request an early breakfast box and settle the bill if convenient.

    Save your international train ticket offline.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 16:30–17:30

    🍽️ Café break

    Rest with coffee and cake before sightseeing.

    Recommended: KaffeeKlatsch Brig.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 17:30–18:45

    📸 Brig old town

    Walk through the historic centre and see Stockalper Palace, its courtyard when accessible, Sebastian Square and old streets.

    A full palace tour is unnecessary.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 19:00–20:30

    🍽️ Dinner

    Enjoy a relaxed Valais-style dinner.

    Recommended: Restaurant Channa. Alternative: Schlosskeller for a more refined setting.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 20:30–21:15

    ⏱️ Prepare for Italy

    Pack fully, organise passport, tickets, Milan hotel details, ferry schedule screenshots and daypack.

    Set two alarms and confirm the Brig departure platform.

    📍 Open in Google Maps

Day 3 Mountain Safety

Altitude and weather can change quickly above Zermatt — follow these rules without exception.
SituationAction
Headache, dizziness, nausea or unusual breathlessnessDescend by train and do not continue hiking.
Wet or slippery trailReturn to Rotenboden and take the train.
Matterhorn hidden but summit otherwise safeContinue for the railway and glacier experience, but reduce photography time.
Thunderstorms forecastDo not hike between Rotenboden and Riffelberg.
