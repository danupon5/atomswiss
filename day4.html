Day 4 · Sat, 1 August 2026

Brig → Milan → Varenna → Bellagio → Milan

Simplon railway to Milan, Lake Como day trip via Varenna and Bellagio

Hour-by-Hour Plan

  • 05:30–06:00

    NOWNEXT🍽️ Wake and breakfast

    Eat your breakfast box, complete checkout and check the international train status.

    Keep passport and ticket in an easily accessible pocket.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • Approx. 06:10

    NOWNEXT🥾 Walk to Brig station

    Leave Hotel de Londres and walk to the station.

    Arrive at least 20 minutes before the train.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • Preferred 06:41–08:50

    NOWNEXT🚂 Brig → Milano Centrale

    Take the earliest suitable direct or one-change international train via the Simplon route.

    Backup working target: approximately 07:44–09:50. Confirm exact times in SBB.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 08:50–09:20

    NOWNEXT🏨 Store luggage in Milan

    Use the staffed luggage facility at or near Milano Centrale. Photograph the receipt and note closing time.

    Keep passport, valuables and medicines with you.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • Approx. 09:20–10:24

    NOWNEXT🚂 Milan → Varenna-Esino

    Take a direct Trenord train. The journey is approximately 65 minutes.

    Avoid the last carriage because Varenna's platform can be short.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 10:24–10:45

    NOWNEXT🚂 Station → waterfront

    Walk downhill from Varenna-Esino station toward the lake and ferry terminal.

    Remember that the return walk is uphill.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 10:45–11:00

    NOWNEXT🚂 Check ferry arrangements

    Locate the ticket office, check Bellagio departures and purchase tickets when possible.

    Confirm which queue is for your boat.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 11:00–12:15

    NOWNEXT⏱️ Varenna walking tour

    Walk the Passeggiata degli Innamorati, harbour, Piazza San Giorgio, Church of San Giorgio and old lanes.

    Keep track of time because the town is steep and lanes can be confusing.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 12:15–12:40

    NOWNEXT🍽️ Café stop

    Have a short coffee break near the waterfront.

    Recommended: Varenna Caffè Bistrot. Espresso, cappuccino, pastry or cold drink.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 12:45–13:45

    NOWNEXT🍽️ Lunch in Varenna

    Have a one-hour lakeside or old-town lunch. Tell the restaurant you need to leave on time.

    Recommended: Al Prato. Alternative: Il Cavatappi. Reserve in advance.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 13:45–14:20

    NOWNEXT🚂 Return to ferry terminal

    Walk back, check the departure board and join the correct queue.

    Arrive 20–30 minutes before the ferry.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • Working target 14:50–15:05

    NOWNEXT🚂 Ferry Varenna → Bellagio

    Cross the central lake and enjoy views from the outdoor deck.

    Keep a light jacket accessible because it can be breezy.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 15:05–15:25

    NOWNEXT📸 Bellagio waterfront

    Walk around Piazza Mazzini and the lakeside promenade.

    Photograph the Varenna and mountain views.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 15:25–16:00

    NOWNEXT📸 Salita Serbelloni

    Climb the famous stepped street and photograph the lake from the upper section.

    Walk carefully on polished stone surfaces.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 16:00–16:35

    NOWNEXT📸 Bellagio old town

    Explore Via Giuseppe Garibaldi, the Basilica area, boutiques and lake viewpoints.

    Avoid shopping too long because ferry queues can build.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 16:35–16:55

    NOWNEXT🍽️ Gelato break

    Have a quick gelato or cold drink.

    Choose takeaway rather than a seated café.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 16:55–17:15

    NOWNEXT🚂 Return to ferry terminal

    Join the return queue early.

    Confirm that the boat stops at Varenna.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • Working target 17:25–17:40

    NOWNEXT🚂 Bellagio → Varenna

    Take the ferry back to Varenna.

    Keep the official ferry timetable screenshot available.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 17:40–18:10

    NOWNEXT🥾 Walk to Varenna station

    Walk uphill to Varenna-Esino station.

    Allow at least 15–20 minutes and do not stop for another meal.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • Approx. 18:30–19:35

    NOWNEXT🚂 Varenna → Milano Centrale

    Take a direct evening Trenord train.

    Do not rely on the final train of the evening.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • Approx. 19:35–20:00

    NOWNEXT🏨 Collect luggage

    Retrieve your suitcase from storage immediately.

    Check the receipt and all belongings before leaving.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 20:00 onward

    NOWNEXT🍽️ Milan hotel check-in and dinner

    Travel to your hotel, check in and have a flexible dinner nearby.

    Use a restaurant close to the hotel or the Milano Centrale food hall to avoid crossing the city late.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
