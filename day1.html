Day 1 · Wed, 29 July 2026

Lucerne → Grindelwald First → Kleine Scheidegg → Lauterbrunnen → Grindelwald

Luzern–Interlaken Express, First Cliff Walk, Wengernalp Railway, Staubbach & Trümmelbach Falls

Hour-by-Hour Plan

  • 05:15–05:30

    NOWNEXT🍽️ Wake up and prepare

    Complete final packing. Check the Grindelwald First and Kleine Scheidegg webcams, SBB connections and weather. Wear walking shoes and carry a warm layer, rain jacket, water, sunscreen and power bank.

    Ask the Lucerne hotel the previous evening for a packed breakfast.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 05:30–05:50

    NOWNEXT🚂 Hotel → Lucerne station

    Check out and walk or take transport to Lucerne station. Reach the station no later than 05:50.

    Eat part of the packed breakfast at the station or on the train.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 06:06–07:55

    NOWNEXT⏱️ Luzern–Interlaken Express

    Take the direct scenic train from Lucerne to Interlaken Ost. The route passes Lake Sarnen, Lake Lungern, Brünig Pass, Meiringen and Lake Brienz.

    Sit on the right-hand side initially, although views change sides. No panoramic surcharge is required.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 07:55–08:05

    NOWNEXT🚂 Change at Interlaken Ost

    Follow signs for the Bernese Oberland Railway. Do not leave the station during the short connection.

    Use station coffee or food shops only when the connection allows at least 15–20 minutes.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • Approx. 08:05–08:40

    NOWNEXT🚂 Interlaken Ost → Grindelwald

    Take the direct train to Grindelwald, approximately 35 minutes.

    Keep your camera accessible as the Eiger becomes visible near Grindelwald.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 08:40–09:05

    NOWNEXT🏨 Hotel luggage drop

    Walk to your Grindelwald hotel, leave the main suitcase and carry only a daypack. Confirm evening check-in arrangements.

    Do not wait for the room to be ready.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 09:05–09:20

    NOWNEXT🥾 Walk to First gondola station

    Walk approximately 10–15 minutes along Dorfstrasse to the Grindelwald First valley station.

    A local bus is possible, but walking is normally simpler.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 09:20–09:50

    NOWNEXT🚂 Gondola to First

    Ride Grindelwald → Bort → Schreckfeld → First.

    Look back over Grindelwald Valley during the ascent.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 09:50–10:10

    NOWNEXT📸 First arrival and viewpoint walk

    Walk directly toward the cliff walk before larger crowds arrive.

    Avoid stopping at the restaurant immediately.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 10:10–10:40

    NOWNEXT⏱️ First Cliff Walk

    Complete the cliff-side walkway, suspension section and viewing platform.

    Allow additional time for photographs of the Eiger, Wetterhorn and Schreckhorn.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 10:40–11:15

    NOWNEXT📸 First View and summit area

    Explore the main viewpoint and surrounding panorama. Wait briefly when clouds are moving, as peaks may reappear.

    Skip Bachalpsee because the return hike would make Lauterbrunnen too rushed.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 11:15–11:50

    NOWNEXT🍽️ Early lunch at First

    Have a quick lunch or coffee at the mountain restaurant.

    Recommended: First Mountain Restaurant. Order soup, rösti, sandwich, coffee or cake.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 11:50–12:20

    NOWNEXT🚂 Gondola descent

    Return by gondola to Grindelwald village. Check the next Kleine Scheidegg train during the descent.

    Leave First by 12:00 at the latest.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 12:20–12:40

    NOWNEXT🥾 Walk to railway station

    Walk directly from the First gondola station to Grindelwald railway station.

    Buy water or a snack only if time permits.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • Approx. 12:45–13:20

    NOWNEXT🚂 Grindelwald → Kleine Scheidegg

    Take the Wengernalp Railway up beneath the Eiger.

    Sit near a window and keep your camera ready.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 13:20–14:05

    NOWNEXT⏱️ Kleine Scheidegg

    Walk around the station, photograph the Eiger north face and views toward Mönch and Jungfrau.

    Backup snack: Bergrestaurant Kleine Scheidegg or Restaurant Eigernordwand.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • Approx. 14:10–15:00

    NOWNEXT🚂 Kleine Scheidegg → Wengen → Lauterbrunnen

    Take the scenic cogwheel train downhill. Remain on the train through Wengen unless a change is required.

    Look down toward Lauterbrunnen Valley and its waterfalls.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 15:00–15:25

    NOWNEXT📸 Lauterbrunnen village

    Walk from the station through the village toward Staubbach Falls. Photograph the waterfall, chalets and cliff walls.

    Do not climb behind Staubbach Falls because Trümmelbach is the priority.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 15:25–15:40

    NOWNEXT🚂 Bus to Trümmelbach Falls

    Take Bus 141 toward Stechelberg and get off at Trümmelbachfälle.

    Keep your pass or bus ticket ready.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 15:40–17:00

    NOWNEXT⏱️ Trümmelbach Falls

    Allow 75–80 minutes for the lift, internal galleries and lower sections. Walk carefully because surfaces are wet.

    Wear shoes with good grip. Admission is usually separate from standard rail passes.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 17:00–17:20

    NOWNEXT🚂 Return bus

    Take Bus 141 back to Lauterbrunnen station.

    Leave the falls by approximately 17:05.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • Approx. 17:20–18:30

    NOWNEXT🚂 Lauterbrunnen → Zweilütschinen → Grindelwald

    Travel back to Grindelwald, changing at Zweilütschinen when required.

    Check the train destination carefully because services divide toward different valleys.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 18:30–19:15

    NOWNEXT🏨 Hotel check-in

    Check in, collect luggage, shower and change.

    Keep the break short so you can still enjoy the evening.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 19:30–21:00

    NOWNEXT🍽️ Dinner

    Have a relaxed dinner in central Grindelwald.

    Recommended: Barry's Restaurant, Bar & Lounge. Reserve around 19:30. Try Swiss dishes, grilled meat or rösti.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 21:00–21:30

    NOWNEXT🥾 Evening village walk

    Walk along Dorfstrasse and look for sunset or evening light on the Eiger.

    Buy water, fruit and snacks for the next morning.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
  • 21:30 onward

    NOWNEXT⏱️ Prepare Day 2 route

    Search in SBB: Grindelwald → Zermatt via Kandersteg. Pack most of your luggage.

    Entering “via Kandersteg” prevents the app from routing you through the faster base tunnel.

    📍 Open in Google Maps
